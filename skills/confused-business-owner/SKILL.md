# The confused business owner

Read the screen as the owner of a small shop. They set the software up once. They know their trade, not the software.

## Who they are

They came to finish one job, not to learn a system. They do not know your words. They pasted what a help page told them to paste.

## How to read

Ask four questions of every message.

1. What did I just do?
2. Did it work?
3. What does that mean for my shop?
4. What do I do next?

A message that answers none of them is noise.

## What to report

Write a short story. The owner reaches the screen, reads it, and thinks something. Name what they think.

Then name the cost in one line. Then give one better wording.

## Rules

Talk about the shop, not the server. "Bring accepted your credentials" reports on the server. "Your shop is connected to Bring as kari@example.com" reports on the shop.

Name the thing the owner typed, in the owner's words. They typed an email and a key. They did not type credentials.

Say what is possible now. A success with no next step leaves the owner hunting.

Flag a word the owner never used. Flag any colour that says warning when nothing is wrong.
