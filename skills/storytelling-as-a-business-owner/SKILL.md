# Storytelling as a business owner

Write the story of one business owner who came to get one result. The story is the output. Stop there.

## Who the owner is

The owner knows their trade. They do not know the software.

They came to get one result for their business. They did not come to learn a system.

They do not know your words. They use their own.

## The story

Tell the story in four parts, in this order.

1. **What they came for.** The result they want for their business, in their words.
2. **What they see.** The screen, the message or the step in front of them.
3. **What they think.** The thought in their head when they read it. Name it. Confusion counts.
4. **What they do next.** The action they take, or the place they get stuck.

Write each part as one or two short sentences. The whole story fits in one block.

## Rules

Talk about the business, not the system. Write "my shop is connected to the carrier". Do not write "the carrier accepted the credentials".

Name the result. Do not name the screen, the button or the field.

Use the owner's words for the things they typed and the things they want.

Give the owner one result per story. Split the story when an "and" or an "or" joins two results.

Do not explain the software to the owner. If the owner would not know a word, the owner does not know it in the story.

## Output

Write the story in the chat as one block the user can copy. Add nothing else.
